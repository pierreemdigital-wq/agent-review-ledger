# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent while requiring evidence for technical claims, give actionable feedback, and keep discussions focused on the project. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially to the extent possible. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

## Attribution

This policy is a concise project-specific code inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1. The full Contributor Covenant is available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
